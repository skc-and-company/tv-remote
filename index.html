Control your TV from this phone

Your TV’s remote on your phone is Samsung’s free SmartThings app — the official remote for this TV, with a touchpad, keyboard, volume and apps.

Open SmartThings Get SmartThings

First time only

1Open SmartThings and sign in with your Samsung account.
2Make sure this phone is on the same Wi-Fi as the TV. Your TV usually appears by itself — if not, tap + → Device → TV → Samsung.
3Tap the TV, then Remote. If the TV asks to allow your phone, choose Allow.
